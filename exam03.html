Information of Midterm Exam #3

  • Coverage: All topics (7, 8, and 9) covered this week (t-confidence intervals, one-sample normal and t-tests hypotheses).

  • Time: The exam will be administered through D2L. You may begin anytime between 12:00 PM Friday and 9:30 PM Sunday. Once you start, the D2L timer will begin counting your 120-minute exam period. You must submit before time expires, as the system will automatically close the exam after 120 minutes.

  • Format: The exam consists of about 35 multiple-choice questions. Each student will receive a slightly different problem set with similar questions.

  • Multiple Attempts:The exam policy allows you to take the test up to two times. Only your highest score will be recorded in the gradebook. If you choose to retake it, you must complete the entire exam again from the beginning; you cannot redo only the questions you missed or you dit them wrong previously.

  • Length: The exam has a strict 120-minute time limit. No extensions will be granted, as you are given a 2.5-day window to start the exam. If you have requested accommodations through the Office of Educational Accessibility (OEA), your exam duration will be automatically adjusted based on the recommended time in your accommodation letter.

  • Logistics: To ensure you have the full 120 minutes to complete the exam, prepare a formula sheet in advance and have a scientific or graphing calculator ready.

  • Make-up Exam.Due to the 2.5-day window to start the exam, make-ups are not offered. A make-up will be considered only with a timely, WCU-approved written excuse, as outlined in the syllabus. Emails requesting to re-open an exam without this documentation will be ignored.

  • Exam Access Instructions: Complete the following steps to locate your exams in D2L:
    • 1. Log in and go to your D2L course page.
    • 2. Click on the Course Icon.
    • 3. Click on Assessments from the menu.
    • 4. Click on Quizzes.
